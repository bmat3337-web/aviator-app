# SPRIBE Adapter Gate

The production adapter is intentionally not implemented until authorized SPRIBE technical documentation, sandbox credentials, commercial authorization, wallet/settlement semantics, and certification requirements are received.

Required implementation inputs:
- Provider API/authentication contract
- Round/event transport and ordering guarantees
- Bet placement and cash-out semantics
- Settlement/callback retry behavior
- Currency/minimum/maximum stake rules
- Wallet model and reconciliation requirements
- Sandbox certification procedure
- Production credential issuance

Until then, the SimulatorProvider remains the only development provider.
