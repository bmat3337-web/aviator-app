# Aviator Regulatory & Market Readiness Matrix v1.0

## Purpose

Track jurisdiction-specific launch evidence without treating any single licence as blanket authorization for other markets.

## Candidate architecture

Curaçao is retained as a candidate licensing route only. It is not a committed launch jurisdiction and does not authorize access to every target market.

## Market-access rule

No jurisdiction is considered launch-ready until its own legal/regulatory evidence is recorded. A Curaçao licence, if eventually obtained, must not be represented as blanket authorization for Zimbabwe, South Africa, the UK, the EU, or any other market.
